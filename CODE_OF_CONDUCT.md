# Community standards

Treat people respectfully, assume good intent, and give feedback about the work. Welcome questions and contributions from people with different levels of experience. Respect privacy and avoid posting someone else's private information.

The upstream project publishes a Code of Conduct with its reporting and enforcement process. Follow that process for community conduct reports. [Upstream Code of Conduct](https://github.com/sukruburakcetin/mapillary-explorer/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
