# Explore street-level imagery from your map

Mapillary Explorer connects an ArcGIS Experience Builder map to Mapillary imagery. Open a street-level capture, compare dates, and record what you observe.

[Open your first capture](../docs/getting-started.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[Install the widget](../docs/installation.md){ .md-button }

</div>

## Choose your path

<div class="grid cards docs-paths" markdown>

-   **Explore imagery**

    Browse captures, apply filters, inspect signs and objects, and compare two dates with Time Travel.

    [User guide](../docs/user-guide/README.md)

-   **Record and review observations**

    Work with Local notes or use a workspace configured by your organization to review shared observations.

    [Workspace guide](../docs/user-guide/workspaces.md)

-   **Configure an experience**

    Connect a Map widget, set appearance and navigation defaults, and review deployment requirements.

    [Publisher guide](../docs/publishing.md)

-   **Develop or contribute**

    Learn how the runtime connects map and viewer events, and run the checks for your change.

    [Developer guide](../docs/widget-development.md)
